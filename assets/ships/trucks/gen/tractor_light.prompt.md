# Ciągnik lekki — kurier portowy (tractor_light)

Output: `tractor_light.png` — 1536 × 1024 px (3:2), przezroczyste RGBA.

- Referencje (dołącz): wybrany kadr kierunku `rig_direction.png` (styl), `assets/container_ship.png`, `assets/inter_station_shuttle.png` (styl cywilny gry).
- Dzisiejsza bryła w demie ciągników: 26,5 × 17 j. (z gondolami 24 j. szerokości), do 2 naczep. Proporcje w prompcie są robocze — szukamy kierunku, liczby dopasujemy do obrazu.
- Dysze MAIN: 2. Siodło (tarcza sprzęgu) na osi z tyłu musi być całe widoczne — na nim gra kładzie oko dyszla naczepy.
- Sam ciągnik, bez naczepy. Lampy namalowane jako zgaszone szkła (światła i strugi rysuje gra).

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent PNG sprite for a top-down 2D space trading and combat game. Reference images: the attached civilian ship sprites set the art style — match their hand-painted realistic hard-surface look, crisp dark outlines, graphite structure with light-gray panels, grime and wear in the seams, small amber marker lenses and their detail density, but design a NEW vehicle. Create exactly ONE compact CAB-OVER space truck tractor, a port courier unit, seen from perfectly overhead in strict orthographic plan view, nose pointing exactly RIGHT (+X), engine nozzles facing LEFT (−X), bilaterally symmetric about the horizontal centerline. This is a spacecraft, not a road vehicle: NO wheels, tires, axles or mudguards anywhere. Silhouette short and stubby, about 1.15 : 1 (length : beam). It must read instantly as a small truck cab seen from above: a flat-nosed cab occupying the front half, with a dark wraparound windshield band across the nose, a chunky bumper with two headlight lenses (unlit glass), two side mirrors on short arms and one round amber beacon dome (unlit) on the cab roof; behind the cab a short open dark chassis deck with two small tanks along the frame rails; one compact thruster nacelle on each side of the chassis, a little wider than the cab, each ending in a ribbed nozzle drum (dark inside); small red tail lenses at the rear corners. On the centerline at the rear of the chassis deck sits a large round coupling turntable (a fifth-wheel plate with a V-shaped slot opening toward the rear), fully visible and unobstructed — the game draws trailers separately and couples them there. Draw the tractor ALONE: no trailer, no drawbar, no cargo. The cab bodywork is painted in ONE flat livery color, medium signal blue, with two white stripes; do not use blue anywhere else. Everything else is neutral graphite, bare steel and light gray. Composition: landscape 3:2 canvas (1536×1024 px), vehicle centered, spanning about 85 percent of the canvas height. One solid, compact, connected silhouette with no thin antennas. Soft even overhead lighting baked into bevels, no cast shadow outside the vehicle. True transparent background with real alpha (not a painted checkerboard), no stars or scenery, no exhaust flames, no engine glow, no lit lamps or light halos, no text, letters, numbers, logos, badges or insignia, no perspective or isometric tilt, no visible side walls.
